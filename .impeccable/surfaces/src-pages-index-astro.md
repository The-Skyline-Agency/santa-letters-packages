# Packages landing page (src/pages/index.astro)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: parents and grandparents on a phone in the evening, late November, choosing a Santa letter package. Action: pick one of five tiers. Proof: real product photos, the North Pole street address, four reviews, the 60 day guarantee, the delivery promise. Constraints: tiers 2 to 5 have no confirmed price, order buttons are placeholder links, structure follows santamail.com/package.

History: the static design was approved on 2026-11-12 ("everything is approved"). The first motion pass (depth-shifted flat plates) was rejected the same day: the user asked for no gradient at the top, type that matches the paper, real three.js interaction, visible parallax, lights and moving objects, and more of the paper itself. This contract reflects that correction.

Unresolved: bundle prices, cutoff date, checkout target, sample video link.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the letter's route. It starts at the North Pole post office at night and ends at the child's front door, and the five packages are five houses on Santa Claus Lane. It refuses the red banner with a three-card pricing grid. (A slim red delivery-promise strip above the hero was part of the approved static design and stays.)

OWN-WORLD: Real layered cut paper. One flat fir green (#1e4a3c) with no gradients, cream paper snow in stacked scalloped sheets with cast shadows, terracotta roofs, amber window light. Aleo, a slab serif, set light for display, cut from cream paper with a lit edge and a shadow; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for reading. Every surface is a sheet with fibre grain: stacked sheets, tags on string, gabled house cards with chimneys, a ruled ledger, taped notes.

STORY: The visitor learns every package starts with a real letter mailed from North Pole, Alaska; sees what each tier adds; trusts it arrives by December 24th; picks a house.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A three.js paper diorama fills the hero on flat fir green: separate cut-out houses, firs and props stand at their own depths on a scalloped snow shelf, ornaments hang on strings, a sleigh crosses the sky, snow falls. One light casts their shadows and follows the pointer; the camera moves with pointer and scroll. Logo on a hanging tag top left, nav right, headline "Santa Letter Packages" centred in the sky with one sentence and the primary button "Choose a Package". The trust facts sit on a paper ticket tucked under the shelf (as approved in the static design).

FORM: The mail route (candidate 6 of 7; seed 4b87a551). Signature interaction: the route stage pins and a paper envelope walks the plank path with a red ink trail while the four steps light in turn, on a wide plate on desktop and a tall plate on phones. Pieces in the scenes answer hover and click; the mailbox and post office post a letter. Raises: from the split-flap board, comparison columns never move and the tier order is fixed everywhere; from the ikebana alcove, the sky stays empty so the village reads.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
