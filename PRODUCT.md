# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro, GSAP with ScrollTrigger (user's choice). Static landing page. Order buttons are placeholder links for now; the checkout target is undecided.

## Users

Parents, grandparents, aunts and uncles in the US and Canada buying a Christmas surprise for a child, usually on a phone, usually in November and early December. They want to pick a package quickly, trust it will arrive before Christmas, and often send to several children at different addresses.

## Product Purpose

Santa's Letter Factory (santaletters.org) sells personalized letters from Santa, custom printed with the child's name and mailed with a real North Pole, Alaska postmark. This page sells the letter in five package tiers. Success is a visitor choosing a tier and starting an order.

## Positioning

The letter is physically mailed from 580 South Santa Claus Lane, North Pole, AK 99705, printed on fine linen paper with a raised embossed seal. Priced per letter with no limit on letters, and each letter can ship to a different household.

## Operating Context

Seasonal purchase with a hard delivery deadline. Each letter is custom printed and hand packed (allow 3 business days). The buyer fills a short form per child: name, optional friend for the Nice List, delivery address.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Five tiers, from Regina's brief, all priced per letter:

1. The Letter: $19.99. Personalized Letter from Santa, Nice List Certificate with golden seal, Santa's Map to your house, Santa's Nice List with the child's name (plus a friend), official North Pole envelope.
2. Letter + Gold: The Letter plus Santa's Gold Christmas Package (North Pole Snow, Reindeer Food, Door Hanger, Santa Stop Here Window Sticker, Nice List Certificate Fridge Magnet). Price not confirmed.
3. Letter + Christmas Eve: The Letter plus Santa's Christmas Eve Package (Christmas Ornament, Milk Mug, Cookie Plate). Price not confirmed.
4. The Full Christmas: The Letter plus both packages. Marked most popular on the preview. Price not confirmed.
5. The Ultimate: both packages plus the Personalized Santa Video. Price not confirmed.

Personalized Santa Video: $19.95 on its own. 30 seconds from Santa, Mrs. Claus or an Elf; includes the child's name, hobbies, pets and friends; delivered within 24 hours by text or email; HD download. From the sister site santavideo.org.

Shipping: Ground $7.94 per letter, FedEx 2-Day $9.99. Shipping and shipping protection are added at checkout. Buyer can ship as soon as possible or pick a send date.

Undecided: bundle prices for tiers 2 to 5, the order cutoff date (the old site says December 15, the preview says "confirm cutoff date"), and where the order buttons lead.

## Brand Commitments

Logo: "Santa's Letter Factory" with the Toys for Tots train (reference/preview-site/logo-menu.png). Existing site uses a script display face (Kitten) and Open Sans on Christmas red. Voice is warm and plain: "keep the magic alive", "the real deal".

User-pinned visual direction for this page: layered paper-cut, 3D diorama Christmas village, in the manner of the four reference images supplied (dark green night village with scalloped snow shelf, cream alpine paper relief, black and white paper sculpture). Structure modeled on santamail.com/package.

- Say what is in the box, plainly, before asking for the order.
